How to play
  • Fill all 54 tiles with 1 → 54 so each number touches the next. Neighbours are the 4 tiles up/down/left/right, including across cube edges.
  • Gold chutes link two tiles that must hold consecutive numbers. Once your path enters a chute, it has to go through.
  • There is exactly one solution.
  • Drag to orbit. Click an empty tile to place the next number. Click a number to continue the chain from it.
  • Use + / − to build the chain forwards or backwards: with − on, clicking 25 suggests 24.
  • Right-click or Shift-click erases. With a tile selected, type digits to set it and use arrow keys to move.
