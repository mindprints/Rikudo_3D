How to play
  • Fill every cell with 1 → N so each number touches the next. Hexagons have 6 neighbours and the 12 pentagons have 5. You can also block the pentagons.
  • A gold chute bridging two cells means they hold consecutive numbers. Once the path enters a chute, it has to go through.
  • Engraved numbers are given. There is exactly one solution.
  • Drag to roll the sphere. Click an empty cell to place the pen number. Click a number to build on from it, and click it again to flip between building up (+) and down (−).
  • Red means a number breaks a rule right now, and the Rule check box says which one. A number can also be wrong without breaking a rule yet.
  • Hint checks against the solution. It fixes one wrong number of yours first. Otherwise it reveals the next number in your direction (+30 s). Shift-click Hint shows the solved sphere for 3 s (+60 s).
  • Shift-click Eraser (Shift+E) clears every number of yours that isn't on the solution path.
  • Right-click or Shift-click a cell to erase it. Arrow keys move the selection, and F flies to your chain end.
